Free Skill Building & Certifications

Build computer skills and earn certificates online, for free.

Digital Skills

The Basics

  • Learn Free: Basic Computer Skills

    Work through the whole series, or pick single lessons. Each one is 6 minutes or less.

  • Learn Free: Internet Courses

    Internet tips, how to search better, staying safe online, and more.

  • How to Add a Digital Signature with Free Tools

    A step-by-step video on signing documents online. About 8 minutes. Video by Leila Gharani.

Typing

  • TypingClub

    Simple lessons that teach good typing posture. Start with a typing test to get placed at the right level.

  • Typing.com

    Typing lessons, tests, and games. For 10-key, try the numeric keypad lesson.

  • Medical Typing Practice

    Practice typing common medical terms. You can also try medical typing tests.

Google Workspace

  • Google Skills: Google Workspace

    Lessons and activities from Google for Workspace, Gmail, Calendar, Meet, Drive, and Sheets.

  • Google Docs Help

    Google's official help and how-tos for Google Docs.

  • Learn Free: Google Courses

    Lessons on Chrome, Slides, Drive, Docs, Gmail, and more.

Microsoft Office

  • Learn Free: Word Foundations

  • Alison: Microsoft Essentials

    Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.

  • Alison: Microsoft Word for Beginners

    An in-depth Word course.

Free Classes & Certifications

100% Free Learning Websites

Every class and certificate on these websites is free.

  • Learn Free

    Mostly computer and software skills, plus job search and AI skills.

  • Alison

    Courses with certificates and diplomas.

  • Calbright College

    Online community college courses and programs. Free for California residents.

  • HubSpot Academy

    Training and certifications for careers in marketing, sales, and customer service.

  • Next Gen U

    Classes and certificates for health care workers.

  • Cursa

    A website and phone app with free courses and certificates in many subjects.

Websites with Some Free Classes

Most classes on these websites cost money, but many are free.

  • Coursera: Free Courses

    Classes and certificates in many subjects.

  • edX: Free Classes

    Courses in almost any subject.

  • Udacity: Free Programs

    A large list of technology courses.

  • Salesforce Trailhead

    All Salesforce training is free. Most certification exams cost money.

Find More Free Certifications

These websites list free certifications in fields like:

  • IT: digital tools, AI, web design, databases, cybersecurity, and coding
  • Marketing, sales, and customer service
  • Office work, communication, and project management
  • Finance, economics, and business
  • Nonprofits, giving, and human rights

  • Class Central: Free Certificates

    The largest list of free certificate courses online.

  • Free Certifications Database

    Free certifications in IT, cybersecurity, databases, project management, digital marketing, and more.

  • Per Scholas

    Free tech training and certifications.
