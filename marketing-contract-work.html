Marketing Your Contract & Gig Work

Find local customers if you do contract work, gig work, or odd jobs.

Nextdoor

Nextdoor is a website and app for your neighborhood.

  • Make a business page. Keep everything in one place: your services, contact info, photos of your work, and reviews. Neighbors can message your business through the app.
  • Watch for people asking for help. People often post asking for recommendations. Others comment to recommend someone, and workers comment to offer their services.
  • Comment before you send a private message. Let them know you sent a message. Many people don't check their inbox, or don't answer messages when they don't know what they're about.

Facebook Groups

Join local Facebook groups where people ask for help:

  • Nevada County Peeps
  • Penn Valley Peeps
  • Nevada County Odd Jobs
  • Let's Get Hired Nevada County
  • Watch for posts from people looking for help.
  • Comment on the post to offer your services. Then send a private message with details and your rate.
  • Say in your comment that you sent a message, so they know to check their inbox.

Create a Google Business Profile

A free Google Business Profile puts your business on Google Maps and in Google searches. You can add your website and contact info.

Learn how to set up a Google Business Profile

Make a Simple Website

A website shows your work, describes your services, and tells people how to reach you. It helps you:

  • Show up in internet searches
  • Advertise your services in one place
  • Link to your Google Business Profile

Free website builders:

  • Google Sites: 100% free, and you can connect your own web address.
  • Canva: free to build. You need a paid plan to connect your own web address.

Other Apps to Try

  • Thumbtack
  • FlexJobs (may charge a subscription)
  • Instawork
