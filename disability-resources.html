Disability Resources

Free help with workplace accommodations, job support, job boards, funding, and skill building.

Workplace Accommodations

  • Job Accommodation Network (JAN) (opens in a new tab)

    The leading source of free, expert advice on workplace accommodations.

    • Talk with an expert: call (800)526-7234, Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern (6 AM to 3 PM Pacific), or use the online contact form (opens in a new tab)
    • Look up accommodations by disability, limitation, or job. Try the A to Z list or Situations & Solutions.
    • Make a free login to save accommodation ideas

Job Support

  • PRIDE Industries Employment Support (opens in a new tab)

    Job coaching, job readiness, and job placement. Services in English and Spanish.

    • Helpline: (844)426-2253, Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM
    • Apply online (opens in a new tab)

  • Ticket to Work (opens in a new tab)

    For people ages 18 to 64 who get Social Security disability benefits and want to work. Connects you with job search help and other job services.

    • Helpline: (866)968-7842 or TTY (866)833-2967, Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 8 PM Eastern (5 AM to 5 PM Pacific)

  • Lime Connect (opens in a new tab)

    Join a free network of peers with disabilities. Connect with partner companies and get career resources, coaching, and scholarships.

  • CareerCircle (opens in a new tab)

    Free career help for people with disabilities and veterans. Search for jobs, build a resume, practice interviews, and join virtual workshops. Get 1-on-1 coaching from recruiters.

Job Boards

PRIDE Industries and CareerCircle (above) also have job boards.

  • disABLEDperson Job Board (opens in a new tab)

    Disability-friendly jobs across the country, in person and remote.

  • DiversityJobs (opens in a new tab)

    Make a login to upload your resume and save jobs.

  • Department of Rehabilitation Hot Jobs (opens in a new tab)

    Jobs at partner businesses across the country, in person and remote.

  • PRIDE Industries Jobs (opens in a new tab)

    A local job board for people with disabilities.

  • Our Ability Job Board (opens in a new tab)

    Upload your resume, make a profile, and get matched with jobs that fit your life and skills.

Money for School & Business

  • disABLEDperson Scholarship (opens in a new tab)

    Money for school.

  • Verizon Small Business Digital Ready (opens in a new tab)

    Finish two free online courses, and you can apply for special grants.

  • CA Small Business Loan Match (opens in a new tab)

    A tool that matches business owners with lenders.

  • Lime Connect Scholarships (opens in a new tab)

    Scholarships for college students and high school seniors, plus internships and other opportunities.

  • ABLE National Resource Center (opens in a new tab)

    An ABLE account is a savings account for people with disabilities who qualify. You can save money without losing most needs-based federal benefits. You can use the money for disability-related costs, including school and work.

Starting a Business

  • SCORE (opens in a new tab)

    Free help starting a business: mentoring, classes, templates, and how-to guides, online and live.

  • Synergies Work (opens in a new tab)

    Connects entrepreneurs with disabilities to resources, networks, and communities to start and grow a business.

Build Skills for Free

  • CareerCircle Courses (opens in a new tab)

    Online courses in computer skills, AI, communication, customer service, project management, Google and Microsoft programs, job search, and more.

  • Salesforce Workforce Navigators (opens in a new tab)

    Scholarships to earn Salesforce Administrator and Associate certifications.

  • BIT Academy (opens in a new tab)

    Remote training and support for a career in Salesforce administration. When you finish, you may qualify for a registered apprenticeship.
